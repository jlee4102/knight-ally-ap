The weekly cycle, and the rules
    Where the numbers come from

    Earning is automatic. Chielo writes every closed check-in to the guild AP sheet — one row per event with its turnout, plus a running AP total per member. This page reads those tabs; nobody types a point in.

    Spending is not. An officer adds a row to AP_LEDGER when someone wins an item: date, member, item, qty, ap, note. ap is negative for a purchase or a void, positive for a correction. Balance is what Chielo credited minus what the ledger took away, capped at —.

    Fixed-price items always cost their listed price, but an auction winner pays their own bid — so the ap column is what counts, and item links the row to the loot board for checking.

    Officers · build the loot post

    For officers, after the Guild Dungeon run. Tick what came out of storage, set quantities, copy the post into Discord. Prices and auction minimums come from the rules, so they are never typed wrong.

    Drop a screenshot of the storage tab here to pre-tick the list. It reads the item names and guesses — always check what it ticked before you copy.
