    Where the numbers come from

    Nothing is typed twice. The Chielo bot closes each boss check-in in Discord, the totals are entered on the alliance loot sheet, and this page reads that sheet and does the AP arithmetic.

    New fortnight? Nothing to do. The page takes the rightmost Att column of each kind, so a new period column appears here on its own. Currently reading —.

    Other guilds on the sheet carry a tag like [Horde] or [DM]; Jollibee members have none, which is how this page tells them apart. Set GUILD_TAG in index.html to show a different guild, or to 'ALL' for the whole alliance.

    Only Cave and WD have columns on the sheet. Crusade, GvG, Guild Dungeon and Epic Dungeon pay AP in the rules but are not counted in standings until they get a column — and because the sheet holds a fortnight total rather than a row per kill, caves turnout tiers cannot be applied. Every kill pays the flat rate.

    Rates, prices and rules read live from ap_rules.json. Roster reads live from the 102 Knight Ally loot sheet. Disputes go to officers; the attendance log is the record.
